# Decision log

HyprAero's decision log, moved out of `AGENTS.md` section 10 so the always-loaded instructions stay small. Newest last. One line per decision: date, decision, reason. Read the relevant entries (grep for the topic) before changing a rule, and append new ones here.

- 2026-10-10 - Rebuild the desktop shell on Quickshell instead of AGS + Astal, restyled in Windows Aero. Reason: owner's decision; the old stack is the thing being left.
- 2026-10-10 - Shared rules live in a portable kit (`.aero/`), the project `AGENTS.md` only adds to them, `CLAUDE.md` imports both. Reason: the shell is the base of a family of apps (the mail client and more); one copy-paste must carry the principles, code style, visual style and family rules everywhere.
- 2026-10-10 - Style is taken from the mail client's tokens (Aero light, Slate dark); the old shell contributes behaviour only. Reason: owner's request - style and design are separate things.
- 2026-10-10 - QML code style is the TypeScript style carried over as far as QML's syntax allows (owner confirmed; the rule is general for any language without its own section). Reason: one style everywhere.
- 2026-10-10 - Identifier case follows the host: `app.hyprAero.shell` for app ids, `hypraero-<module>` for layer namespaces and config folders. Reason: owner's request, Hyprland configs use lowercase kebab.
- 2026-10-10 - Bar defaults to the bottom and its position is a setting; placement, sizes and behaviour are configurable, the Aero look is not. Reason: owner's rule "full control while user-friendly" within the family identity.
- 2026-10-10 - Run with `qs --path=./`; checks proposed as `qmlformat`, `qmllint` and `node --test` for logic kept in plain JS. Reason: Quickshell has no build step; decisions kept out of QML are testable without the shell.
- 2026-10-10 - Workspace switching dispatches the Lua form `hl.dsp.focus({ workspace = N })`, isolated in `services/Workspaces.qml`. Reason: Hyprland 0.56 with a Lua config rejects the old `workspace N` text; legacy `.conf` hosts would need a second implementation behind the same function.
- 2026-10-10 - First version is only the bar with workspace buttons; the glass blur fallback token and reduced-transparency support are postponed. Reason: owner asked for the minimum first.
- 2026-10-10 - Workspaces 1-10 are always shown, as the owner's Hyprland binds exactly those. Reason: matches the old bar and the shortcuts; more appear only while they exist or have focus.
- 2026-10-10 - Theme follows the system through the freedesktop portal (`gdbus` call + monitor, parsed in `services/colorScheme.js`), not Qt's `styleHints.colorScheme`. Reason: Qt reported no preference on this setup; the portal is what GTK and browsers follow. Without gdbus or a portal the bar stays Aero light.
- 2026-10-10 - Workspace icons: apps are ranked by their biggest window (`services/workspaceList.js`), icons come from the desktop entry, then an icon named like the window class, then a drawn generic window. Reason: owner's rule from the old bar; the fallback keeps apps without an icon visible instead of blank.
- 2026-10-10 - The current-workspace marker is one highlight that slides and stretches (leading edge faster than the trailing one, 120 / 220ms), instead of the old bar's physics bubble. Reason: soft, not bouncy (`.aero/STYLE.md` motion), and it reads as one object moving.
- 2026-10-10 - Bar height 48px. Reason: owner asked for a bit higher; room for 28px icons plus a 16px badge.
- 2026-10-10 - Hover popup lists a workspace's windows (not one row per app), biggest window first, ordered in `services/workspaceList.js`. Reason: owner asked for the list sorted by size; titles tell two windows of one app apart. Closing, dragging and name overrides from the old bar are not built yet.
- 2026-10-10 - The hover popup is Windows 7 style thumbnail cards with live previews (Quickshell `ScreencopyView` on the window's Wayland handle), anchored to the bar's top edge instead of the button. Reason: owner asked for the original taskbar look and for no overlap with the bar. Cards are not clickable yet, so they have no hover highlight.
- 2026-10-10 - The preview popup window is created once, as wide as the bar, and never moves or resizes; only the glass panel inside glides between buttons (input mask limited to the panel). Window sizes for previews come from Hyprland, not from `ScreencopyView`, which reports none. Reason: re-placing a popup window made it flash in the screen corner, and the capture view stayed blank without an explicit size.
- 2026-10-10 - Preview cards keep a fixed picture height (120px) and take their width from the window's aspect ratio, clamped to 64-280px (16:9 until the size is known). Reason: owner asked; a fixed 200px width left bars around tall and wide windows.
- 2026-10-10 - A window hanging even 1px off its monitor (`hangsOffMonitor` in `services/workspaceList.js`) gets no live preview: each time the popup opens it captures one frame, reads it once through a hidden `Canvas` (256x256 copy) and crops to the box around the drawn pixels (`services/pictureBounds.js`); the card takes that part's proportions. Windows fully on screen stay live and are not scanned. Reason: Hyprland captures the whole window but leaves what it did not draw transparent, where that part lands does not follow the window's position (a probe showed a window off the bottom drawn in full), and a live picture of such a window slides during workspace animations; the owner chose a frozen, correct frame over a live one there.
- 2026-10-10 - The preview popup keeps two card sets (`modules/bar/PreviewCards.qml`): moving to another workspace loads its cards in the hidden set, keeps the old ones on screen until every new picture has arrived (at most 500ms), then crossfades (220ms); the set fading out stops capturing and is emptied afterwards. Reason: owner saw a blink of empty cards between workspaces; at most two sets capture at once, and only during a switch.
- 2026-10-10 - App icons are drawn through `design/AppIconImage.qml`: loaded at 256px and shrunk by the GPU with mipmaps. Reason: the icon theme shrinks a 256px-only icon (Vivaldi, Discord, Kitty) to the asked size in one step, which left jagged edges; compared side by side on screen, the mipmapped version matches a smoothly scaled file.
- 2026-10-10 - Closing from a preview card goes through the window's own Wayland handle (`toplevel.wayland.close()`, foreign-toplevel close), not a Hyprland dispatcher. Reason: it is the same polite request as the window's close button (apps can ask to save), and it can only reach that window; a probe showed `hl.dsp.window.close` with an argument it does not understand closes the active window instead.
- 2026-10-10 - Clicking a preview card dispatches `hl.dsp.focus({ window = 'address:0x...' })`, which switches the workspace too, and leaves the pointer where it is. The address is checked against `^0x[0-9a-fA-F]+$` before it goes into the Lua text. Reason: the owner tried moving the pointer to the middle of the window and found it a bad idea.
- 2026-10-10 - `ControlButton` takes its hover from a `HoverHandler` instead of the `MouseArea`. Reason: the close button sits inside the card button, and the card must stay lit (and the close button shown) while the pointer is on it.
- 2026-10-10 - The right-end strip goes to the lowest listed workspace without windows, or the one after the highest when all have windows (`firstEmptyWorkspace`); clicking it again does not bring the windows back as on Windows. Reason: owner's request; workspaces replace minimising, so "show desktop" means an empty workspace.
- 2026-10-11 - Dragging a preview card onto a workspace button moves the window there, as a real Wayland drag (`Drag.Automatic` from a `DragHandler`, the card grabbed as the drag picture, a `DropArea` on each button) carrying the address in the private format `application/x-hypraero-window`. Reason: the popup and the bar are separate surfaces and the pointer belongs to the popup while the button is held, so only a compositor drag reaches the bar and draws the card under the pointer; a private format means dropping on an app does nothing.
- 2026-10-11 - The move dispatches `hl.dsp.window.move({ workspace = N, window = 'address:0x...', follow = false })`; addresses pass `isWindowAddress` (`services/workspaceList.js`) first. Reason: a probe on Hyprland 0.56 showed `silent = true` (the old bar's form) is ignored and the view follows the window; `follow = false` keeps the view, which is what dropping a window away should do.
- 2026-10-11 - While a card is dragged the popup stays open and its cards are not rebuilt (`dragging` in `WorkspacePopup.qml`), then it re-reads the windows and closes unless the pointer is on it or a button. Reason: the card is the drag's source; destroying it mid-drag (the popup closing because the pointer left, or a window list change) would break the drag.
- 2026-10-11 - While a window is dragged, every screen gets a transparent full-screen overlay layer (`modules/bar/DropLayer.qml`, namespace `hypraero-drop`, shown only during the drag) that draws the card and takes the drop; the popup hides (kept alive, input mask emptied). The card is not the compositor's drag icon. Reason: owner asked for the card to stay where it was grabbed, and Hyprland 0.56.2 ignores the icon's hotspot (Qt sends `wl_surface.offset`, then `attach(buffer, 0, 0)`, whose 0, 0 overwrites the offset in Hyprland's attach handler); drops outside our own surfaces (over apps) could not be caught either. A press on the layer removes it, so a drag that ends without notice can never leave the screen covered.
- 2026-10-11 - Where a drop goes is `dropWorkspace` in `services/workspaceList.js`: within 48px of the bar over the buttons the pointer counts as on it (closest button), and within 240px beside them (left: the leftmost free workspace, right: the rightmost), further away the lowest free one; no free one means nothing happens. The target button is lit during the drag. Reason: owner's rules for the sides and for "up" (lowest free chosen by the owner); the lit button makes the outcome visible before the drop.
- 2026-10-11 - New kit token `drag-image-opacity` (0.75, both themes) for the dragged card. Reason: owner approved; an opaque card would hide the lit button under it.
- 2026-10-11 - The sides of the bar catch a drop up to 240px above it, the buttons only 48px. Reason: owner asked for a much taller area at the sides; over the buttons it stays short so "up" (lowest free workspace) is still reached after a short move.
- 2026-10-11 - Dragging a workspace button moves the whole workspace, with the same drop places and lit target as a card (`modules/bar/DragSource.qml` shared by both, state in `services/BarDrag.qml`). When the target number is free the workspace is renumbered (`hl.dsp.workspace.change_id`), otherwise its windows are moved one by one; `workspaceMovePlan` in `services/workspaceList.js` decides. Reason: owner asked to keep order, positions and sizes; renumbering keeps the workspace object itself (probe: same position and size after the change), while a move re-tiles each window. Hyprland refuses a taken id and an empty workspace exists only while shown, so the empty workspace on screen is first left for the source (probe: Hyprland drops it, the renumber then succeeds).
- 2026-10-11 - Moving the workspace on screen leaves the view on its old number, as moving a single window does. Reason: one rule for both drags, "you stay where you are".
- 2026-10-11 - The bar re-reads the workspaces on Hyprland's `changeworkspaceid` event. Reason: Quickshell 0.3.2 does not handle it; a probe showed the renumbered workspace's windows vanishing from the bar until a refresh.
- 2026-10-11 - The workspace buttons are not rebuilt during a drag (their count holds still). Reason: a button is the drag's source; rebuilding it mid-drag would break the drag.
- 2026-10-11 - `DragSource` sits under its button's `MouseArea` (z -1). Reason: placed above it, the `DragHandler` swallowed every click (owner found clicking a workspace with windows and a preview card did nothing; reproduced headless, a press and release never reached `clicked`); under it, clicks pass and a drag still takes over once the pointer moves. Covered by `tests/qml/tst_DragSource.qml`.
- 2026-10-11 - QML components that need only Qt Quick and services are tested headless with `qmltestrunner` against fake services (`tests/qml/fakes/`). Reason: the click bug above could not be caught by `node --test`, and testing it must not need the shell to run.
- 2026-10-11 - After a renumber (`changeworkspaceid`) the windows and monitors are re-read at once, and workspace numbers never come straight from Quickshell's objects: a placeholder with id -1 is numbered by its name (`workspaceNumber`), a window falls back to Hyprland's record of it, and "which workspaces exist" is the ones with windows, shown on a monitor or focused (`existingIds`), not Quickshell's list. Reason: owner saw both slots empty and the marker on the first button for seconds after a workspace move. Quickshell 0.3.2 matches workspaces by id, so the renumbered one is unknown to it: a window / monitor refresh links to a placeholder named after it with id -1, its QML workspace refresh can neither create nor drop workspaces (and answered before the placeholder existed in a probe), and the old object stays. Probe after the fix: the window showed on its new button within 0.4s.
- 2026-10-11 - Every drag out of the bar leaves a ghost where it was taken from (the picture at `drag-ghost-opacity` in a control frame, drawn by `DropLayer`); it gets the button hover face under the pointer, no workspace button is lit there, and a drop on it does nothing. The origin travels in `BarDrag` as the bar's screen plus a rectangle in bar coordinates (the popup reports its window's place in the bar, `barOffset`). Reason: owner's request, so a drag started by mistake can be undone; the hover face says "letting go here does something".
- 2026-10-11 - New kit token `drag-ghost-opacity` (0.35, both themes). Reason: owner chose the faded picture over an empty slot.
- 2026-10-11 - The ghost is a drop zone instead of a faded picture: a glass slot (`design/DropZone.qml` on `GlassPanel`) with the flat undo arrow (`design/ReturnGlyph.qml`) and "Put back" when it fits; lit, its edge takes the `focus-glow` ring and glow. The compositor frosts it through a blur rule on `hypraero-drop` (README.md); without the rule it is plain drawn glass. `drag-ghost-opacity` is removed from the kit again, unused. Reason: owner preferred a real ghost, after Vivaldi's tab drop zone (frosted, outlined, a label saying what the drop does), in Aero style.
- 2026-10-11 - The launcher is a Windows 7 start menu opened by an orb at the bar's left end (`modules/startmenu/`), its layout flipped for a bottom bar: sections stack up from the search field (featured closest to the orb, the dark line, every other app above), rows inside a section read top to bottom; the list is a bottom-to-top `ListView` of reversed rows, so a short list hangs from the search field. Reason: owner's request and choice of order; the old launcher's "Featured" / "Most used" sections and stars carried over.
- 2026-10-11 - Apps rank by frecency (`services/appUsage.js`): +1 per use, the score halves every 14 days, a use counts at most once per 10 minutes per app, records below 0.05 are dropped on read. A use is a launch from the menu or a window keeping focus 10s (`countFocus`, a property until the config file exists), never while the user is idle 5 minutes. Reason: the old raw counter never forgot (last year's habits beat this week's), only saw launcher launches and needed a halving hack at 1,000,000; owner chose launches + focus.
- 2026-10-11 - Featured apps and usage live in `$XDG_STATE_HOME/hypraero/start-menu.json`, validated on read (bad entries dropped and logged). Reason: they are state, not configuration; a broken file must not lose the rest.
- 2026-10-11 - Scores are taken when the menu opens (`Apps.refreshScores`), not live. Reason: an open menu must not reorder under the pointer when a use is counted.
- 2026-10-11 - Apps start with `Quickshell.execDetached` on the desktop entry's parsed `command` (Quickshell strips field codes, probe on this machine); terminal apps through `xdg-terminal-exec`, then `$TERMINAL`, then the first common terminal, the arguments handed to `sh` as parameters. Not Hyprland's `exec_cmd`. Reason: no shell or Lua quoting of desktop-entry text, and the process outlives the shell.
- 2026-10-11 - The orb is drawn (`design/StartOrb.qml`) from new kit tokens ("Start orb" in `.aero/STYLE.md`), the mark is Hyprland's own path (`design/HyprlandMark.qml`, BSD-3-Clause, notice kept in the file). No picture of the Windows orb is shipped; a reference PSD may only be measured for values. Reason: owner asked for the Windows 7 orb with a Linux mark; the shell is Hyprland-only, so the Hyprland mark (owner's choice); Microsoft's artwork must not end up in the repository.
- 2026-10-11 - The menu is a `PopupWindow` with `grabFocus`; one menu open at a time (`services/StartMenuState.qml`), a click on the orb within 300ms of the menu closing does not reopen it. Reason: the popup grab gives it the keyboard (probe: window active, search focused on open) and closes it on a click outside, which arrives before the orb's own click.
- 2026-10-11 - Shut down, Restart and Log out ask first in a task dialog (`design/TaskDialog.qml`), Sleep and Lock do not. Lock runs hyprlock when installed, otherwise `loginctl lock-session`; Log out dispatches `hl.dsp.exit()`. Reason: only leaving closes the user's apps; lock needs a locker, which the shell does not bring.
- 2026-10-11 - `ControlButton` takes keyboard focus (focus-glow ring and glow, Enter / Space press it). Reason: the task dialog's first button must have focus, as in Windows 7.
- 2026-10-11 - A section's header sits under its apps (from the search field up: "Featured", featured apps, the dark line, "Most used", the other apps; a search's "Apps (n)" under its results); rows inside a section still read top to bottom. Reason: owner's request, the menu is read from the bottom.
- 2026-10-11 - Each app row has a second line, the description or else the program (`appDetail` in `services/startMenu.js`). Reason: owner's request, as the old launcher did.
- 2026-10-11 - The orb is 50px, 3px above the screen's bottom, rising 5px above the 48px bar as in Windows Vista (first 56px rising 10px; owner found it stood out too much), in its own `PopupWindow` of the bar (`modules/startmenu/StartButton.qml`); its input region is the orb's ellipse plus the bar's corner under it, and the menu is lifted so its bottom sits on the orb's top. Reason: owner's request; a layer surface cannot draw outside itself and a popup is always above its parent, while growing the bar's window would move every coordinate the drag and preview code takes from the bar's height.
- 2026-10-11 - The bar's glass has no left and right border: its panel is 2px wider than the screen on each side. Reason: owner's request, the bar should look as if it runs on past the screen's edges.
- 2026-10-11 - The dark line sits above each section's header instead of between the sections: from the search field up, "Featured", the line, the featured apps, "Most used", the line, the other apps (a search: "Apps (n)", the line, the results). Reason: owner's request, each header then reads as the label of what is above the line.
- 2026-10-11 - An app dragged out of the start menu starts on the workspace it is dropped on, with the drop places and lit target of a window drag (`DragSource` with `appId`, format `application/x-hypraero-app`); the view stays. Reason: owner's request, so an app is not always opened on the current workspace; one gesture for everything dropped on the bar.
- 2026-10-11 - A dropped app is started by Hyprland (`hl.dsp.exec_cmd(cmd, { workspace = 'N silent' })`, built by `startOnWorkspace` in `services/workspaceList.js`), not `Quickshell.execDetached`; a click still uses `execDetached`. Reason: Hyprland can tie the window of the process it started to the workspace before it is shown (probe: opened there, the view stayed). The command is shell-quoted, then made a Lua string with every quote, backslash and control character escaped; tests run it through real `lua` and `sh`.
- 2026-10-11 - Besides that, for 30s after a drop (5s after its first window) every new window of the app is moved to the workspace (`services/launchPlacement.js`, matched by desktop entry; two drops of one app give one window each, oldest first). Reason: owner found drops did nothing for their apps: Hyprland only knows windows of the process it started, and `code`, Electron apps, Flatpaks and a browser already running open their window from another process. The window shows on the current workspace for a moment first; that was the owner's suggestion.
- 2026-10-11 - `DragSource` moved from `modules/bar/` to `design/`. Reason: the bar imports the start menu, so the start menu importing the bar's folder would make the two modules import each other.
- 2026-10-11 - While an app is dragged, the start menu stays open and shown, its row left empty in place (no rebuild) with the "Put back" ghost over it, and its input mask emptied so the drop layer gets the drop; a drop anywhere on the menu puts the app back (`returnArea` of `DragSource`, `BarDrag.returnRect`), lit like the ghost. It closes only after a start. Reason: owner's request; dropping on the menu can only mean "never mind". Closing the drag's source window would end the drag. The menu draws the ghost and the dragged picture over itself (`StartMenu.qml`, from the drop layer's pointer passed by the bar), the drop layer's copies lying under it in the same places. Reason: Hyprland 0.56 draws the bar's popups above every layer, the overlay drop layer too (owner saw the picture go under the menu).
- 2026-10-11 - The config file exists: `~/.config/hypraero/config.json` (`$XDG_CONFIG_HOME`), JSON with one object per group, only what differs from the defaults; read by `services/Config.qml`, validated by `config/settings.js` (wrong kind or unknown name: default and a log line, never a crash), re-read live when it changes, appears or goes (the folder is created at startup: a file appearing in a missing folder is not noticed). No settings window yet. Reason: owner asked for the first setting; JSON because the old shell's config and the state file are JSON.
- 2026-10-11 - Setting `startMenu.touchControls` (default off): on, dragging the app list scrolls it and a 400ms hold picks an app up (the press becomes the drag; a click still starts); off, a drag on the list never scrolls it and a row is picked up as soon as it moves. `countFocus` moved into the file (`startMenu.countFocus`). Reason: owner's request; scrolling and picking up by the same drag fought each other, the hold is how phones separate them.
- 2026-10-11 - The app list's scrollbar works: it widens and takes the hover border colour under the pointer, its thumb can be dragged, a press beside it brings it there, the wheel works on it; in both control modes. Reason: owner's request; with touch controls off it is the way to scroll by pointer.
- 2026-10-11 - QML tests can load the real design files: `tests/qml/fakes/qs/design/qmldir` points at them, `tests/qml/fakes/Quickshell/` stands in for Quickshell's `Singleton` so `Theme.qml` loads, and fakes for `SystemTheme`, `AppIcons` and `Apps`. Reason: the app list's behaviour lives in the real `AppList` / `AppRow`; a look-alike test would not catch a change there.
- 2026-10-11 - When the start menu's popup is dismissed during an app drag from it, or within 500ms after (`regrabWindow`), it is shown again at once without reset or fade; only the bar's close after a start closes it. Reason: Hyprland ends the popup's focus grab when a drag out of it ends, put back included (run and observed: dismissed about 35ms after the drag, shown again about 35ms later); without this the menu vanished and the next press went to the window behind it (owner: "I can't take it again").
- 2026-10-11 - The orb is 54px and centred on the bar's height: it rises 3px above the bar and 3px run off the screen's bottom (drawn past its popup's bottom edge), with a soft `icon-shadow` under it. Reason: owner found the previous one (50px, 3px above the screen's edge) off-centre and looking pressed into the bar; part of it may be cut by the screen, it must sit on the bar's middle and stand on it.
- 2026-10-11 - The start menu reads top to bottom: "Featured" and the featured apps, a dark line, "Most used" and the other apps with a score, another dark line, the rest A to Z (`menuRows` in `services/startMenu.js`); a search's "Apps (n)" header sits over its results. A part with nothing in it leaves out its header and its line, except "Featured", which keeps its hint. This replaces the bottom-up order and the header-under-apps decisions above. Reason: owner's request, the old launcher's layout.
- 2026-10-11 - An app's desktop actions open under it in the start menu like a tree branch: an arrow at the row's right end (right of the star, which keeps its column), the actions on a line from the app's icon and lined up with its name, lit like rows, no icon of their own. Keyboard: Up / Down walk through them, Tab (always) and Right / Left (with no search text) open and close. Reason: owner's request, after the old launcher's tree whose actions read as part of the app; the arrow is on the right as the owner asked, the tree line is what keeps them "inside" the app. Repeating the app's icon on them would make them look like apps.
- 2026-10-11 - Pinned apps, after Vista's Quick Launch: right of the orb between two dotted grips (`design/GripGlyph.qml`, glass edge colours), tool buttons with the app's icon, launchers only (a new copy per click). Stored as desktop entry ids in `$XDG_STATE_HOME/hypraero/pins.json` (`services/pins.js` validates, places and removes; `services/Pins.qml`); an id without an installed app is kept and hidden. Reason: owner's request; state, not configuration, like the featured apps.
- 2026-10-11 - A drop on the pins (on the bar, between the grips) pins the dragged app before the gap closest to the pointer, marked by a glowing line (`focus-glow`): an app from the start menu, a window of an app with a desktop entry (the window stays), or a pin (it moves; dropping it next to itself changes nothing). A workspace is never pinned, so the pins are not a place for it and the side rule applies; a window of an app without a desktop entry dropped there does nothing. A pin dragged elsewhere starts its app on a workspace like a start menu app. Reason: owner's request ("the same drag and drop"); one gesture for everything dropped on the bar, the mark makes the outcome visible as the lit button does.
- 2026-10-11 - `BarDrag.from` says what a drag was taken out of ('pins', 'startMenu', '' for the bar). Reason: an app id alone cannot tell a pin from a start menu row, and the drop and the empty slot depend on it.
- 2026-10-11 - Right-click menus of bar things (a preview card, a pin) are one focus-grabbing `PopupWindow` of the bar (`modules/bar/BarMenu.qml`) sized to the menu, opening above-right of the pointer; the previews stay open while it is open and close after it unless the pointer is back on them. Reason: a popup that grabs may only sit on the bar or on another grabbing popup, and the previews do not grab; drawn inside the previews it could not close on a click elsewhere.
- 2026-10-11 - A preview card's menu: Focus (bold, what a click does), Fit into layout, Float freely (the one that applies is enabled), Pin to bar / Unpin from bar, Close. Floating goes through `hl.dsp.window.float({ action = 'set' | 'unset', window = 'address:0x...' })` in `services/Workspaces.qml`; Hyprland's own record of the window says whether it floats. Reason: owner's request; names say what happens in plain words instead of Hyprland's "tile" / "float". Probe on Hyprland 0.56.2: `set` and `unset` worked on a test window; an unknown action toggled it, so only the two are ever sent.
- 2026-10-11 - A pin's actions are in its right-click menu, indented under the app on a tree line (`indent` items of `MenuPanel`), with "Unpin from bar". Reason: a bar icon has no room for an arrow; the tree line repeats the start menu's look so the actions still read as part of the app.
- 2026-10-11 - The bar's icon-only controls get their tooltip from a small popup of the bar (`modules/bar/BarTooltip.qml`, the family `Tooltip` inside, pointer passing through), placed only once the pointer has rested. Reason: the bar is too low to hold a tooltip, and a popup moved while shown flashes.
- 2026-10-11 - The start menu's scrollbar owns the pointer from the press to the release: the app rows stop where the scrollbar starts (no row's drag handler ever sees a press there), it keeps its press (`preventStealing`), and while held the thumb keeps the size it had when taken and stays where the pointer puts it, the list following it; a thumb at either end asks the list for that end by name. Reason: owner saw the thumb shrink under the pointer and the drag "fall off" into picking up an app. The thumb was sized and placed from the list's estimated height, which changes as rows get built (featured 50px, apps 42, headers 26); and every row's drag handler watched presses under the strip and could take one over once a row scrolled far enough under the pointer (the old bottom-up order hid it, pointer and rows moving opposite ways).
- 2026-10-11 - `AppRow.qml` has no inline components. Reason: under Quickshell 0.3.2 both of its inline components failed with "is not a type" (the whole shell kept the last good config), while `qmltestrunner` loaded them; `PlacesColumn`'s inline component still works, so the cause is not known. Collapsed action rows are also hidden, not only clipped: clipping does not keep the pointer away from them.
- 2026-10-11 - Closing a window from the shell (`Workspaces.closeWindow`) first stops every live preview of it (`closingAddresses`, held 10s), then sends the close.
