# Decision log

The decision log of HyprAero's notifications module. Newest last. One line per decision: date, decision, reason. Read the relevant entries (grep for the topic) before changing a rule, and append new ones here.

- 2026-10-24 - Notifications are a HyprAero module of their own: the `notifications` branch, started without history, with the `design` branch as a submodule at `design/`, run as its own `qs` process. Reason: owner's request: it must install and run by itself, without the bar's code or resources, and still look like the bar (`design` branch's `DECISIONS.md`, same day).
- 2026-10-24 - The server is Quickshell's `NotificationServer` in `services/Notifications.qml`; for now it only logs a line about each notification and keeps none. Reason: a skeleton that proves the module runs on its own, before the owner and I plan the balloons. Run and observed on a private session bus (`dbus-run-session`, offscreen copy): the name is taken, `GetServerInformation` answers `quickshell`, both test notifications were logged, and the app was told `NotificationClosed` with reason 2 ("dismissed") at once, because a notification that is not kept is dropped; the balloons will keep them. A second copy started meanwhile logged that the name was taken and that it would try again once free.
- 2026-10-24 - The log line about a notification (`services/notificationLog.js`) holds its app, urgency, the lengths of its summary and body, its number of actions and whether it has a picture, never its words. Reason: a notification can carry a private message or a one-time code, and secrets never appear in logs (`.aero/PRINCIPLES.md` section 4); the lengths are enough to see that the text arrived.
- 2026-10-24 - Notifications show as Windows 7's balloons (`design/Balloon.qml`), one at a time with the rest queued, as on Windows 7; `balloons.maxVisible` in the config gives a stack instead (the newest nearest the edge). Reason: owner's request ("exactly like on Windows 7"), and the owner chose the queue with a setting over a fixed stack or a fixed queue.
- 2026-10-24 - Their position is a setting (`balloons.position`: a corner, the middle of an edge or the centre; default bottom right), with `balloons.margin` and `balloons.monitor` (empty: the focused screen). The window is a layer surface (`hypraero-notifications`, layer top, no keyboard) that respects other bars' reserved room. Reason: owner's request: unlike Windows 7, they must not only appear in the bottom-right corner; respecting the reserved room puts them above a bottom taskbar as on Windows 7 without depending on the bar.
- 2026-10-24 - The stem points at the corner the balloon sits in (centred in the middle of the top or bottom edge, none in the middle row or with `balloons.stem` off), on the balloon nearest the edge only. Reason: owner's choice: the module cannot count on the bar's tray icon to aim at, and Windows 7's stem pointed down at the right, where the tray is.
- 2026-10-24 - Settings live in `notifications.json` in the shared config folder, validated like the bar's `config.json` (wrong kind, unknown name, word not in the list or number out of range: the default, and a line in the log). Reason: each module reads and writes only its own files there (`design/AGENTS.md` section 5), and the bar's parser reports unknown groups.
- 2026-10-24 - A balloon stays as long as the app asks; left to the server, `balloons.timeout` (5000 ms, Windows 7's default for notifications); a critical notification left to the server stays until closed. The pointer on it holds it (the time starts again once it leaves); it fades out on expiry, and closes at once on a click, the close button or an action. Reason: the notification standard (critical ones should not expire by themselves) and Windows 7's behaviour. Run and observed on a private bus: the second notification appeared only once the first expired after 5.2 s (reason 1), expired after its own 1 s, and a critical one stayed until `CloseNotification` (reason 3).
- 2026-10-24 - A click on the balloon does the app's "default" action (else dismisses it); the other actions are buttons along the bottom, the first the main one (accent). Reason: freedesktop apps send actions Windows 7's balloons had no room for; the click on the balloon was Windows 7's way to act on one, and the AGS shell's buttons worked. Run and observed: "Reply" sent `ActionInvoked reply` and closed it (reason 2), a click on another sent `default`.
- 2026-10-24 - Words are plain text: the server says it takes no markup, tags sent anyway are removed and entities decoded, and the title and words are cut to 63 and 255 characters with an ellipsis. Reason: Windows 7's balloons were plain text with exactly that room (`NOTIFYICONDATA`'s 64 and 256 characters, ending with a null).
- 2026-10-24 - The standard message icon names (`dialog-information`, `-warning`, `-error`, `-question`, their `gtk-` and `-symbolic` forms) are drawn with Windows 7's own icons (`imageres.dll` 81, 84, 98, 99, copied to `resources/icons/`); other icons come from the app's path, the icon theme or its desktop entry; an attached picture is shown at 32px as Windows' large balloon icon, instead of the small icon. Reason: `design/AGENTS.md` section 4.3 (Windows' own pictures first); `notify-send -i` sends an icon's name as the image (`image://icon/<name>`, run and observed), which is treated as the icon, not a picture.
- 2026-10-24 - Every action is a button, in the app's order, the first the main one (accent), "default" included ("Open" when it has no words), wrapping onto more rows; a click on the balloon still does "default". This replaces the earlier decision that "default" was only the click. Reason: owner's request: a main action, a secondary one and more, as in the old AGS shell. Run and observed: of four actions, the third button sent `ActionInvoked read`.
- 2026-10-24 - The app's icon and the picture sent with a notification show together (icon 16px before the title, picture 48px at the left), and an app without an icon gets the design's generic one. This replaces the earlier decision that the picture took the icon's place. Reason: owner's request to see both; 48px rather than Windows 7's 32px so a picture can be made out.
- 2026-10-24 - `notificationText.js` no longer uses `trimEnd` or `Array.from` on strings, and preparing the words is wrapped so a failure logs only the notification's number and the error's kind. Reason: Quickshell's engine lacks `trimEnd` (and `trimStart`, `replaceAll`, `matchAll`, `at`, `flat`, `flatMap`, `Object.fromEntries`; measured on 0.3.2) and splits strings into UTF-16 units in `Array.from`; a message over 255 characters threw, its balloon stayed empty, and the error, which quotes the string, put the first 254 characters of the message in the owner's log. The text tests now run with node made to lack the same (they failed on the old code), and `tests/engine.test.mjs` checks no library script uses the missing methods.
- 2026-10-25 - Notifications of apps the user unticked on the bar's "Notification Area Icons" page, or of every app with its "Hide all notifications", are not shown: logged as hidden and not kept (the app is told reason 2). The interface is two JSON files in the shared state folder, `notification-apps.json` written here (every app that sent a notification) and the bar's `notification-filter.json`, version 1 (AGENTS.md section 2.1); the app is matched with its tray icon here only. Reason: owner's request and choices (a tick per app, apps without a tray icon listed too, "Hide all" on the page, choices that survive a reboot); neither module knows where the other is installed, so `qs ipc --path` cannot reach it, and files in a known folder are the host's way (`.aero/FAMILY.md` section 4). An app is keyed by its `appName` (else its desktop entry) because that is what every notification carries. Run and observed on a private bus with a scratch copy: an app hidden by its key and one hidden through its tray icon (matched by name, and by desktop entry) were closed at once with reason 2 and logged as hidden, others showed, and turning "hideAll" on in the file hid the next one without a restart.
- 2026-10-25 - `NotificationApps` is made at startup (shell.qml reads it) and reads the bar's file blocking (`blockLoading`) if a notification comes before it was read. Reason: run and observed: a singleton is only made when first read, so the first notification made it and was shown before the filter was read, although its app was hidden.
- 2026-10-25 - The bar's page no longer has a "Notifications" tick: a tray icon's behaviour says whether its app's notifications show ("Hide icon and notifications" and the new "Only show icon" hide them), and an app without a tray icon has "Show notifications" / "Hide notifications". Nothing changes here: the filter file keeps its format (version 1), the bar now works `hiddenTrayIcons` out from the behaviours. Reason: owner's request (the bar's `DECISIONS.md`, same day).
